How it works

Three steps to a planned day

It takes a few minutes in the morning. After that, Dayloom keeps the plan in front of you.

  1. Write down today’s tasks

    Type them in or pick them from your Inbox. For each one, choose how long it is, from Very short to Longest.

  2. Start your day

    Press Start My Day. Dayloom gives every task a time within your work hours, around your meetings and lunch break, and your plan for the day is set.

  3. Follow the Now card

    The task for this moment stays visible in the app, in the menu bar and on a floating panel. Mark it as done and the next one takes its place.

Why Dayloom

A list says what. A calendar says when. Dayloom does both for one working day.

Compared with a to-do list

A to-do list holds your tasks and leaves the timing to you. Dayloom gives each task a time within your work hours, so in the morning you can see whether the day fits.

Compared with a calendar

In a calendar you place every block by hand and move it again when something runs late. Dayloom works out the times from each task’s length and adjusts the rest of the day when you finish one.

Plan and record in one place

The plan, time tracking, requests from your tools and your team’s work are in the same app, so what you planned and what you did can be seen side by side.

How buying works

You choose a plan inside the app, where the price is shown in your local currency. Each payment covers one month of the chosen plan and does not renew automatically. Buying the same plan again extends it by a month; choosing a different plan replaces the current one from that day.

What happens after a month

Nothing renews by itself. When the month ends, your account goes back to Free, and your plans, time entries and files stay where they are. In a team, members other than the owner lose access to team data until a team plan is bought again. Nothing is deleted.

Questions and answers

When can I buy Pro?

Soon. Our payment setup is still being completed, so the paid plans cannot be bought yet. Until then you can use everything in Free, and Pro will open inside the app when payments are ready.

Is Dayloom really free?

Yes. Planning your day, time tracking, the calendar, connections, widgets and rewards are free, with no time limit. The paid plans add planning with AI, Claude Code, smart request detection, reports and team members.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use Dayloom without signing in. You create an account only when you want Pro or a team.

Where is my data kept?

Without an account, your plans, tasks, time entries and settings stay on your Mac and are not sent to us. If you create an account or join a team, your account details and the work shared within your team are stored in our cloud database so that team members can see them. Dayloom also keeps automatic backups of the last 30 days on your Mac.

Read the privacy policy

Does it work offline?

Yes, for your own planning and time tracking, because that data is on your Mac. AI features, team features and the connections to GitHub, Jira and Linear need an internet connection.

Which Macs are supported?

Any Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later, with Apple silicon or an Intel processor.

How do teams work?

One person buys Team Pro (up to 5 people, owner included) or Team Pro+ (unlimited members) and invites the others with a code. Each member joins with their own Dayloom account and does not pay separately; Pro features work for everyone on the team. Admins and managers assign, edit and reassign work, see the team board, mark people as away and can stop or correct a timer. An admin can remove a member and hand their open tasks to someone else, and sets the team’s time zone. Everyone keeps planning their own day.

How does AI use my data?

AI runs only when you use it. For Plan with AI, task prompts and solving with Claude Code, only the text and files needed for that action are sent to Anthropic’s Claude service, and they are not used to train models. Claude Code runs on your own Mac, in the folder you connect. Text from your connected tools is sent to AI only if you turn on Smart detection.

How do I cancel, and can I get a refund?

There is nothing to cancel: each payment covers one month and does not renew automatically. Because a plan is activated right after payment, the right of withdrawal does not apply. We do refund double charges, plans that were not activated within 24 hours, and cases where Pro could not be used because of a technical problem on our side. Write to us within 14 days of the payment.
